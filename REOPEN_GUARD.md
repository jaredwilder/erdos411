# Reopen Guard

This repository is a curated public baseline of a working research corpus, not an
invitation to restart proof search from scratch.

Allowed by default: reproduce, audit, formally harden, bind sources, reduce axiom
footprints, port the ω = 6 and ω = 7 kill-trees into the kernel, improve
certificates, preserve retractions, prepare publication artifacts.

Not allowed by default:

- re-introducing the retracted `cambie_depth3_check` closure claim in any form
  (see `RETRACTION-EG411-R2-2026-06-09.md` - it is authoritative);
- weakening or restating the source target to manufacture a closure claim;
- restarting mathematical attack branches on the residual open question.

Reopening the mathematical target requires the explicit operator directive:
**REOPEN 411**.
